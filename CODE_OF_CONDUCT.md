# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
The **Ship Together** community is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive space for builders of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior
- Be constructive, welcoming, and respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Give credit where it is due and collaborate in good faith.
- Keep feedback focused on code, design, and product ideas—not personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal insults.
- Spam, malicious links, or attempts to compromise accounts or data.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.

## Enforcement
Maintainers of `ship-together-app` may edit, close, or remove content, or restrict access for behavior that violates this Code of Conduct. Report issues privately to the repository maintainers.
